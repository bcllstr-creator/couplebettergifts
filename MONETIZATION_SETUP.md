## Google AdSense: setup steps

1. Prefer to choose and connect the permanent domain first, so the application uses the address you intend to market.
2. Visit https://adsense.google.com/start/ and sign up using your Google account. Applicants must be 18 or older; Google explains alternatives for minors.
3. Add the public website and your own country and payment/contact information. Complete Google's identity checks if requested. Handle banking and identity information directly in Google.
4. Copy the publisher ID (ca-pub- followed by digits) and the website verification code from the Sites setup screen. Send that ID/code to the coding assistant; it is not a password.
5. We will add the verification method, submit a build, and you can request review in AdSense. The current site has a readable catalog, About, and a current Privacy page. Review and improve original content yourself before applying. These pages do not guarantee approval; Google can reject a thin quiz/affiliate catalog.
6. After approval, create one display ad unit initially. Send its generated code (including its data-ad-slot ID) and the ads.txt line Google supplies. We can connect it to a reserved space. Publisher IDs and slot IDs must be your real values.
7. Before serving ads, update the privacy notice for Google advertising cookies and configure applicable consent through Google's supported consent tools. The current notice correctly says there is no ad script yet. Do not enable ads while leaving that statement in place.
8. Check the rendered placement, mobile layout, and reporting. Never click your own ads or ask others to click to support you. Do not repeatedly request new ads at every quiz question. Content-light quiz screens are a weak placement; the readable catalog and recommendation content should be assessed first.

To show Amazon-supplied product images and current prices automatically, investigate Creators API in Associates Central. Amazon's current prerequisites say at least 10 qualifying sales in the preceding 30 days, enrollment in the relevant Associates marketplace, API registration, and credentials. A tracking ID alone is not API access. Do not put API secrets in public catalog.js or share them in chat; configure them through the hosting secret mechanism when eligible. We have not copied Amazon listing photos, reviews, or prices. Another route for images is your own product photographs or manufacturer images with permission; record the rights and use accurate alt text. No synthetic product pictures are used.
