## Asset / implementation notes

- No merchant product photos have been licensed or downloaded in this research. Do not treat public-page access as permission to republish photos. Use the merchant's approved creatives or feed after affiliate approval, or obtain permission first.
- Personalization Mall's program supplies approved image and text links through Impact. Lovepop advertises a product feed through its affiliate program. Miniature Market creatives should come from its approved LinkConnector account; confirm access rather than inventing feed availability.
- Keep these merchant URLs unchanged until approved tracking URLs are available. Do not attach the Amazon tag to them. The catalog should distinguish ordinary retailer links from active commission links.
- These descriptions are original gift-use rationales based on published product details. They are not hands-on reviews or claims about tested durability/quality.
- A better quiz can distinguish: shared activity vs individual gift; practical vs personalized; two players vs group; recipient already plays guitar/uses physical dice; time available for custom production. These distinctions prevent more mismatches than a gender question.
