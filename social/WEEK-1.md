# Organic social: Facebook, Instagram, and Pinterest

Images are in `social/images/`. Use the `-ig.png` files (1080 x 1350) for Instagram and Facebook, and the `-pin.png` files (1000 x 1500) for Pinterest. To make more posts, add an entry to `social/build-posts.mjs` and run it again.

## Account setup (you do this; I cannot create accounts)

1. **Facebook Page.** On Facebook, go to Pages, then Create new Page. Name: Couple Better Gifts. Category: Website, or Shopping & Retail. Add the site link (use the Facebook link from the table below) and the bio text below.
2. **Instagram.** Create the account @couplebettergifts if the name is free, then go to Settings, Account type and tools, and switch to a professional account (Creator or Business).
3. **Connect them.** In Instagram's Accounts Center, connect the Instagram account to the Facebook Page. This lets you post to both at once.
4. **Scheduling.** Open Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com). Its Planner schedules one post to both Facebook and Instagram, so you can set up a whole week at once.

**Bio text (both):** Gift quiz and buying guides. Pick a budget and their interests, then get a short list of gifts to check out.

**Instagram bio link:** https://couplebettergifts.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=gift_quiz&utm_content=bio

Instagram captions cannot hold clickable links, so Instagram posts say "Link in bio." Facebook posts can carry the link. Facebook tends to show posts with outside links to fewer people, so you can also put the link in the first comment instead.

## Measuring it

In Google Analytics, open Reports, then Acquisition, then Traffic acquisition, and change the first column to "Session source / medium". Visits from these posts show as facebook / organic_social, instagram / organic_social, and pinterest / organic_social. The utm_content value tells you which post brought each visit.

Log each week in the Organic Weekly Log tab of the class spreadsheet: posts published, reach (from Meta Business Suite or Pinterest Analytics), link clicks, site sessions, and quiz completions.
