# Activate Amazon affiliate commissions

1. Make the website publicly accessible before applying. The current Site is public at https://goodguess.benjamincallister224.chatgpt.site. A public site should contain useful original gift advice and recent content; Amazon gives roughly 10 posts as a rule of thumb, not a guarantee of approval. The 72 idea cards alone do not guarantee acceptance.
2. Visit https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/ and select Sign up. Sign in with your Amazon account.
3. Enter the payee/contact information requested, and list the full public website URL. Describe the gift quiz and your planned traffic sources honestly. Choose your preferred store ID.
4. Complete the required tax interview and payment setup in Amazon's dashboard. Do not share passwords, tax information, or banking details with the coding assistant.
5. Send only your Associates tracking ID (often ending in -20 for the US program). Set config.affiliateTag in dist/catalog.js to that ID. The site then adds it to both direct product and search links and enables the Amazon disclosure.
6. Review the site and your links in Associates Central. Amazon reviews applications after at least three qualifying sales in the first 180 days; personal orders do not qualify. Acceptance is not guaranteed.
